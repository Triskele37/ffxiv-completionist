Navigation Drawer (that thingy on the left)
This UI shows all top level groups at all times as well as the home menu (FFXIV Completionist)
When you click one of these groups, all of its sub groups expand while the previously selected group's retracts
You can then continue clicking down through sub groups until you get to what you want to look at
Breadcrumbs (that chain of groups just below the overall completion metric)
If you want to jump back up to an earlier parent group, you can click on any of these to navigate backwards
Navigation History (the button to the left of the breadcrumbs)
Several of the groups previous open are cached in this menu
Clicking on one of these navigates to that group
Completion Bars (those pretty bars that show up when a group is selected)
When any group that shows completion bars is selected, you can click on those bars to dive down instead of using the nav drawer
Search
The Search input in the top-left of the page allows you to search the app for a term
After entering the term hit Enter or click the search button to run the search
Clicking on a group in the results takes you directly to that group
The Search input can be focused by hitting Ctrl+F or Cmd+F
Bookmarks
Individual tasks can be added or removed from bookmarks through their row action menu
Groups can be added or removed as well through the "Table Settings" overlay
Only bookmarked tasks are counted toward completion of the Bookmarks group
Bookmarked items are not duplicated in the Overall percentage
Final Note
Your currently selected group is saved when closing the app to help you pick up where you left off :D
There is a "Show All Tasks" button for groups that have similar sub groups under them, this will give you a table with all of the items underneath in one big table
