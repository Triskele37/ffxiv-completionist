**Unreal Trials**

All duty-raid-finder content (typically) derives their keys from ContentFinderCondition.  
Unreal trials are an exception, because only the most recent Unreal retains its data 
in this content group.  
For that reason, The most recent unreal's id at the time of the key remap is used 
for all of them (1007), prefixed with a 9 and incremented/decremented.
